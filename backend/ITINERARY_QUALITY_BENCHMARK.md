# Offline itinerary quality benchmark

Run `node benchmarks/itinerary-quality.js` from `backend/`. The eight scenarios
contain only synthetic places, prices, hours and directed walking legs. The
baseline was captured from checkpoint `0848a7f4` before 4F.2 changes. No
provider adapter or network call is used. The harness reports the top-ranked
plan, all plan rankings, schedule and uncertainty metrics, expansion counts,
feasible option counts, rejection reasons and one-run execution time.
Run `node benchmarks/itinerary-quality.js --hours` for the 4F.3 dated-hours
comparison on the same four-candidate mixed scenario. It replaces the baseline
all-unknown hours with two dated-open venues, one explicitly closed venue and
one unknown venue. Both runs are synthetic and use the existing pure engine.

| Hours comparison | All unknown baseline | Dated-hours aware |
|---|---:|---:|
| Top-plan supported stops | 0 | 2 |
| Closed candidate stops excluded | 0 | 1 |
| Top-plan unknown stops | 3 | 1 |
| Returned valid plans | 6 | 6 |
| Price-and-hours confirmed engine plans | 0 | 2 |
| Provisional engine plans | 6 | 4 |
| Search expansions | 40 | 25 |
| Top-plan useful utilization | 73% | 73% |
| Top-plan category coverage | 2 | 2 |

The confirmed counts above refer to synthetic engine plans with known fixture
prices; the orchestrator still marks every fixture response provisional. The
top plan is unchanged because it did not use the closed venue. In other data,
rejecting a closure can correctly reduce plan availability. Wall-clock timings
are emitted but are one-run measurements and should not be treated as a
performance improvement claim.

Useful utilization is `(visit minutes + directed travel minutes) / available
minutes`. This measures scheduled use, including necessary travel; it does
not judge whether every minute should be filled. Category coverage counts
requested categories in the selected plan. Budget validity means the existing
Budget Engine did not classify a selected plan as exceeding the limit.

The selected mixed plan in B is fictional Food `c` (75 minutes), Food `d`
(75 minutes), then Café `a` (45 minutes), with three eight-minute walking
legs. It uses 219 of 300 minutes, has no waiting, and stays within the
synthetic budget. It is not a real-world recommendation. The two Food stops
show that category coverage is a preference, not a hard equality constraint.

The baseline favoured short plans because its schedule score rewarded unused
time. It also sorted the live shortlist by requested category and ID, allowing
one category to monopolize four slots. The revised ranking rewards visit time,
requested-category coverage and travel efficiency; it removes duplicate
stop-set permutations after sorting. The shortlist now rotates through
requested categories before filling remaining slots. Within a category it
uses only supplied operational status, mood tags and a coarse distance bucket
before stable Place ID. It remains four places,
one Places request and one Routes matrix of at most 20 or 25 elements.

Search work is unchanged in these fixtures because only ranking and
shortlisting changed. The engine still caps expansion attempts at 300,
candidates at 12 and returned plans at six. Single-process timing is emitted
by the harness but is noisy and not evidence of a speed improvement.

## Limits and trade-offs

Short café outings may now prefer two similar cafés to one; coverage and
variety weights reduce this tendency but do not forbid it. When results lack
costs or dated hours, affordability and opening certainty remain unknown;
the orchestrator keeps those plans provisional. The pure-engine H fixture
contains unknown hours and prices; visit-duration assumption warnings are
generated by the orchestrator, which is separately covered by its tests.

The current Places field mask provides no rating or defensible mood relevance
signal. Its normalized candidates have empty tags and no provider relevance
score. The engine's default 50 relevance is neutral, not a fabricated Google
rating. Place-ID fallback order within a category is stable but cannot guarantee that
the best local venue survives the shortlist. No additional Places request or
paid field was introduced. Verified prices, full dated opening hours and
time-dependent route quality remain future data-quality work.
